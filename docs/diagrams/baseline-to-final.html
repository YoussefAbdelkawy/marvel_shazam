From baseline to final, one change at a time

Each bar adds what one change contributed to top-1 accuracy on Whisper-style noisy queries (dev split, 900 queries). SCORE, the metric every change had to improve, is printed under each step.

Why SCORE jumps at step 3

Saying "no match" is half the job

SCORE = noisy top-1 − 0.5 × how often non-Marvel dialogue gets a Marvel answer. The baseline never said "no match", so it lost the full 0.5. The threshold (step 3) took rejection of non-Marvel lines from 0% to 92.5%, and the calibrator to 99.2%. That is why SCORE rises far more than top-1 there.
