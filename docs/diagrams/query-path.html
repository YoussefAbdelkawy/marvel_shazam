Architecture · query path

How Marvel Shazam identifies a scene

What happens after you tap the shield: speech becomes text, two searches run side by side over every subtitle chunk, their rankings are fused and reranked, and a vote plus a confidence model decides between an answer and “no match”.

Open weights

Every model is open and runs offline

  • Whisper small.en (OpenAI, MIT) via faster-whisper
  • bge-small-en-v1.5 (BAAI, MIT), fine-tuned here on our own train split
  • bge-reranker-base (BAAI, MIT)
  • No LLM and no API: nothing leaves the laptop

Learned on our data

Two small things are trained

  • The fine-tuned embedder (contrastive loss, 9.9k pairs)
  • A logistic regression that turns 13 features into p("the top answer is right")
  • Thresholds per query length, tuned on dev

Not RAG

Retrieval, no generation

It is the "R" of RAG only: it finds the chunks most like what was heard and votes. Nothing is generated, so it can't make up a title.
